# Bundled phone validation

libphonenumber-js 1.13.14 is used with its maximum bundled metadata and mobile examples, shared by the staff renderer and staff API. No remote metadata or flag/font download is required. Country flags use Unicode regional indicators; readable country names and calling codes remain available without flag glyphs. Formatting/structure is not possession verification.
